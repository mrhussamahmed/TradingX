A. Mission Control

Operator-first cockpit. The home screen answers: what is happening now, is it safe, what needs action, and what should I investigate next.

Pros

  • Best for real market-hour use
  • Keeps interventions fast and obvious
  • Performance stays available, but subordinate to operations

Cons

  • Research becomes a secondary workflow
  • Needs stronger information discipline to avoid clutter

B. Portfolio Intelligence

Performance and attribution first. The app behaves like a PM analytics terminal that also exposes operations when something goes wrong.

Pros

  • Best for evidence-driven improvement
  • Benchmarking and strategy attribution feel first-class
  • Great for daily review and post-session analysis

Cons

  • Slower for real-time intervention under pressure
  • Can overemphasize analytics during live operations

C. Modular Workbench

Composable layout system with saved views for operations, risk, and review. Good long term, but heavier to design and implement correctly.

Pros

  • Most flexible over time
  • Can support multiple workflows without forcing one page
  • Scales well if the platform grows

Cons

  • Highest implementation complexity
  • Easy to become messy if defaults are weak
  • Not ideal if we need a strong opinionated cockpit first
