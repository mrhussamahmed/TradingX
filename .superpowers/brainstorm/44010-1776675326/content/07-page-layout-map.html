Page-by-page layout and workspace map

This section defines what the product actually is. Instead of one overloaded dashboard, TradingX becomes three distinct workspaces with a few focused pages each. The goal is to make it obvious where to go for live operation, performance review, or deep investigation.

Mission Control

Default workspace during active operations. Everything here must help the operator decide what to do now.

Home

Market Desk
  • Bot status, broker/feed health, risk budget, open alerts
  • Orders needing attention and positions overview
  • Operator-plus actions: pause, retry sync, recovery, bounded reruns

Orders

Execution Queue
  • Active orders, fills, blocked orders, rejections, age, latency
  • Execution quality snapshot and “what needs action” sorting

Risk

Exposure & Guardrails
  • Gross/net exposure, concentration, overnight exposure, budget usage
  • Intervention events and what control is constraining the bot

Positions

Live Book
  • Open positions, P&L, entry context, holding time, risk status
  • Drill into the latest decision behind each position

Incidents

Operator Alerts
  • Critical/warning/info queue with acknowledgements and next steps
  • No passive noise; every alert must point to an action or reason to ignore

Portfolio

Performance workspace with two internal views: guided by default, analyst on demand.

Home

Performance Summary
  • Goal-oriented summary: is the bot achieving its objective or not?
  • Key metrics with interpretation: good or bad, and why
  • Guided view by default with toggle to Analyst density

Benchmarks

Relative Performance
  • Portfolio vs S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, custom baselines
  • Alpha, beta, excess return, up/down capture, drawdown comparison

Strategies

Attribution & Contribution
  • Which strategies are helping, hurting, unstable, or consuming risk
  • Clear controls like strategy filter, benchmark selector, metric view

Drawdowns

Capital Protection
  • Drawdown timeline, recovery speed, benchmark-relative downside behavior
  • Answer whether the bot is protecting capital better than passive exposure

Diagnostics

Performance Leaks
  • Execution drag, slippage trends, weak signals, blocked profits, regime mismatch
  • Prioritized “what to improve next” backed by evidence

Research

Replay and diagnostics workspace. This is where the operator becomes investigator.

Home

Replay & Diagnostics Hub
  • Entry point for trade replay, decision tracing, and system investigation
  • Top evidence, recent failures, open diagnostic threads

Replay

Trade Narrative
  • Signal, risk gate, selection logic, order path, fill outcome, aftermath
  • Used to answer exactly what happened and why

Evidence

Artifact Explorer
  • Validation reports, incident trails, digests, scheduler logs, summaries
  • Searchable and grouped by purpose, not raw file chaos

Diagnostics

System Health
  • Data lag, feed freshness, runtime state, drift, model freshness, queue backlog
  • Focus on technical root cause analysis rather than live operation

Layouts

Saved Review Surfaces
  • Custom analyst layouts for replay-heavy, diagnostics-heavy, or audit-heavy work
  • Lives here so customization does not pollute Mission Control

Global pages outside workspaces

`Settings`, `Permissions`, and `Data Availability` stay global, not embedded inside every workspace.

Page naming rule

Pages should say exactly what they do. No vague labels like “studio” or “language” without a clear operational meaning.

Navigation rule

The operator always knows whether they are operating the bot, judging performance, or investigating behavior.
