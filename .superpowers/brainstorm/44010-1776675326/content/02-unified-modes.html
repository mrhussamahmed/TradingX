Why this architecture

All three modes exist, but they share the same product so the user is not relearning navigation, permissions, or terminology.

Data rule

Unavailable metrics become hidden or explicitly unavailable. No synthetic fill just to make panels look complete.

Control rule

Operator Plus controls are allowed. Manual trade entry and unsafe config mutation stay out of the main UI.

Visual rule

Thinner charts, calmer contrast, darker shell, less decorative noise, and stronger hierarchy around actions and risk.
